Questions, answered

Which phones support this eSIM?
Most phones from 2018 onward that are carrier-unlocked and eSIM-capable, including iPhone XS and newer, Google Pixel 3 and newer, and most Samsung Galaxy S20 and newer models. Your confirmation email includes a full compatibility check before you activate.
What happens after my 5GB runs out?
Nothing charges automatically. Your data simply pauses, and you can top up with any plan above in the app whenever you're ready. No card is required to claim or use it.
Is the 5GB really free? What's the catch?
No catch. It's one 5GB eSIM on us per person, valid for 30 days from the moment it first connects to a network. We verify one claim per email address to keep it fair for everyone.
Can I make calls and send texts?
This eSIM is data-only. Most travelers stay in touch over WhatsApp, iMessage, or wifi calling through their existing number, all of which work fine over this connection.
What if something goes wrong during setup?
In-app chat support is available if your QR code won't scan or your phone needs a manual entry code instead. Most setup issues are resolved in a couple of minutes.
